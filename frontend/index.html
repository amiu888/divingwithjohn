How It Works

Surface to Depth

A clear, structured progression that keeps every student safe and confident at every stage.

01

Awareness

Understand the physiology of breath-holding — what happens in your body and how to work with it, not against it.

02

Breathing

Master diaphragmatic breathing, recovery breaths, and pre-dive preparation techniques on land and in the pool.

03

Equalisation

Learn Frenzel and mouthfill equalisation to descend comfortably without ear pain at any depth.

04

Pool Training

Static and dynamic apnea sessions in a controlled environment to build confidence and extend breath-hold time.

05

Open Water

Transfer your skills to the ocean — duck dives, constant weight descents, and buddy safety protocols.

06

Depth & Beyond

Push personal records, refine technique, and progress through AIDA levels with ongoing mentorship.
